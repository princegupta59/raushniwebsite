Biography

Raushni Kumari is a visual artist based in New Delhi.

Working across painting, drawing and digital image-making, Raushni Kumari traces where personal memory meets the wider world. Nature is not scenery here, but a living archive: weather, soil, light and gesture become a quiet vocabulary.

After studying Fine Arts, the artist developed a practice rooted in slow looking and material experimentation. Their work has been shown in group and solo exhibitions across India.

Artistic philosophy

“A painting does not answer a question. It keeps it open long enough for us to feel its weight.”
